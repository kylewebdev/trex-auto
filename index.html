Scheduled Maintenance

60 / 90 / 120K service intervals

A scheduled service is crucial to maintaining a dependable automobile. Making your car work harder than it needs to shortens its life and leads to costly repairs down the line. Our maintenance packages cover oil and filter changes, fluid top-offs, tire rotations, belt and hose inspections, and multi-point vehicle checks — all performed by experienced technicians who know what to look for. Staying on top of your 60K, 90K, and 120K milestones keeps your engine running efficiently, protects your warranty, and helps preserve your vehicle's resale value. Drop it off at T-Rex for a maintenance service today.
